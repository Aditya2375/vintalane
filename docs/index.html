SCOPE / THE CLOCK IS ONLY THE START

A pipeline audit.
Not an alpha claim.

Footfall and shipments are fictional aggregate activity proxies. No individual, company, device, vendor feed, location trace or real return is represented.

Source, CLI & full numerical contract ↗

Two clocks, one hard cutoff

For each required proxy, observation ≤ decision, availability ≤ decision, and age ≤ 8 days. Choose greatest observation day, then greatest availability day, then record ID. Equality is eligible. First releases arrive after 1 or 3 days; revisions after 6 or 8. Added delay shifts every release by 3 days, not the observation. The hindsight lens drops only the availability test.

Labels have a clock too

Decision t predicts outcome t+1, available at t+1. Formation decisions 20..139 use labels available by fit clock 140. Test decisions 140..219 have later labels and never update scaling or coefficients. Each lens has its own frozen transform, on the same common complete row IDs.

Missingness is visible

Shipments observations 80..95 and 175..190 are absent. If no eligible proxy is within 8 days, exclude that decision from both lenses and the baseline. No backfill, interpolation or future imputation. Common complete cases improve comparison but may bias the selected sample; added delay changes the cohort.

Construction is not discovery

One generator deliberately shares hidden activity with later outcomes. Another uses independent outcome noise. A third reverses the relationship only in the test. No costs, positions, trades, market validation, causal effect or significance test. Real alternative data also needs lawful sourcing, privacy, rights, release/ingestion logs and representative coverage.
